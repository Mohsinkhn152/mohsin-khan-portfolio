sheet 01 / 03 — appointment booking, dual login

Glow Beauty Parlor

A salon booking app with two separate logins — customers and staff. Customers browse services and bundled deals, then book an appointment against real open time slots, so two people can never land the same slot by accident.

Staff sign in through their own panel to confirm, cancel, or reschedule appointments, and to manage which services and deals customers see.

roleEnd-to-end Flutter build
stackFlutter, Dart
usersCustomer + staff login
the tricky partKeeping booking slots accurate on both sides at once — a slot a customer sees as open has to disappear the moment it's taken, and a staff reschedule has to update the customer's view without either side refreshing manually.

sheet 02 / 03 — multi-restaurant ordering

Restaurant Ordering App

A list of restaurants, each with its own menu grouped by cuisine. Any dish can be added to a running cart, with quantity adjusted right from the item card — no separate cart screen required to make a change.

Checkout collects a delivery address and lets the customer choose Cash on Delivery, card, or JazzCash/Easypaisa before placing the order.

roleEnd-to-end Flutter build
stackFlutter, Dart
flowBrowse → cart → checkout
the tricky partThe cart total has to stay correct no matter which restaurant or screen an item was added from, updating instantly as quantities change without ever reloading the page.

sheet 03 / 03 — inventory management

Hardware & Plumbing Inventory

Built for a shopkeeper to run their own stock. Items live inside categories — Plumbing, Electrical, Tools, Fasteners — and every category shows a running total: how many products, how many units, and their combined value.

Opening a category breaks that total down item by item, with quick controls to adjust stock counts as things sell or arrive.

roleEnd-to-end Flutter build
stackFlutter, Dart
userShopkeeper / admin
the tricky partEvery stock change had to recalculate totals at both the category and item level immediately, so the numbers on the dashboard are always something the shopkeeper can trust at a glance.
